Who We Are

Toronto Metropolitan Aerial Vehicles (TMAV) is a multidisciplinary design team at Toronto Metropolitan University dedicated to pushing the boundaries of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). We bring together students from mechanical, electrical, aerospace, computer engineering, and business disciplines to gain hands-on experience in aerospace design, hardware prototyping, and autonomous flight control.

Our Mission & AEAC Competition

Our mission is to design, manufacture, and deploy an autonomous drone to compete in the annual Aerial Evolution Association of Canada Student Competition (AEACSC). This competition challenges university design teams across Canada to solve complex real-world aerial missions, including autonomous navigation, object detection, and payload delivery.
